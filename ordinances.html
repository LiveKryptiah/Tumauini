Civic Transparency & Participation

How Ordinances Are Enacted in Tumauini.

Standard parliamentary process upholding democratic consultation and accountability.

Step 01 • First Reading

Filing & Referral

Draft proposed by Mayor, Sangguniang member, or citizen initiative; read by title and referred to appropriate standing committee.

Step 02 • Committee Review

Public Hearings

Committee analyzes fiscal and legal impact, conducts barangay stakeholder hearings, and prepares the formal Committee Report.

Step 03 • Final Enactment

Second & Third Reading

Plenary debate and roll-call voting. Upon enactment, submitted to the Municipal Mayor for executive approval and official gazette publication.

Sangguniang Bayan Regular Session

Regular parliamentary sessions are convened every Monday morning at 9:00 AM at the SB Session Hall, 2nd Floor Legislative Building, Municipal Complex. Sessions are open to accredited civic observers, barangay leaders, and student delegations.

Presiding: Hon. Christopher B. Uy Secretary: SB Secretariat Desk
