Municipal Mayor

Mission and vision of Tumauini.

Our long-term blueprint for sustainable agro-industrial progress, heritage preservation, and accountable governance.

Municipal Vision 2030

Our Vision

“Tumauini as a premier agro-industrial and heritage-tourism hub in Cagayan Valley, guided by empowered citizens living in a resilient, green, and progressive community under honest, transparent governance.”

Our Civic Mandate

Our Mission

“To uplift the quality of life of every Tumauineño through sustainable agricultural modernization, world-class heritage stewardship, responsive healthcare and social protection, robust infrastructure, and digital municipal services.”

Institutional DNA

Core values guiding our municipal civil service.

T

Transparency (Katapatan)

Zero tolerance for corruption and open disclosure of public finances.

U

Unity (Pagkakaisa)

Inclusive cooperation across all 46 lowland and upland barangays.

M

Modernization (Kaunlaran)

Harnessing smart digital tools and modern farming technologies.

A

Action-Oriented (Aksyon)

Rapid 24/7 emergency response and expedited citizen frontline services.

U

Upliftment (Kabuhayan)

Prioritizing farmer subsidies, local livelihood, and market access.

I

Integrity (Integridad)

Moral governance, civil dignity, and adherence to legal ethics.

N

Nature Stewardship (Kalikasan)

Protecting river basins, reforestation, and ecological sustainability.

I

Inclusivity (Mamamayan)

Empowering youth, women, senior citizens, and persons with disabilities.

Strategic Horizon

Five development pillars driving municipal growth.

Pillar 01

Agro-Industrial Modernization

Subsidizing hybrid seeds, mechanized post-harvest dryers, and direct farmer-to-market trading facilities.

Pillar 02

Heritage & Eco-Tourism

Safeguarding San Matias Church and boosting community eco-tourism along the Pinacanauan River basin.

Pillar 03

Climate Resilience & DRRM

Reinforcing Rescue 3325 dispatch capabilities and watershed reforestation against typhoon floods.

Pillar 04

Digital & Open Governance

Full financial transparency via online Form 74-A disclosures and electronic permits for all citizens.

Pillar 05

Universal Healthcare & Safety Nets

Expanding 46-barangay primary clinic health access, senior pensions, and educational scholarship grants.
