Application Guidelines & Submission Steps.

Standardized appointment process under the 2017 Omnibus Rules on Appointments.

01

Review Requirements

Verify that your education, experience, and eligibility meet the CSC Qualification Standards.

02

Prepare CSC Form 212

Complete the revised 2017 Personal Data Sheet (PDS) with passport photo and work sheet.

03

Assemble Attachments

Attach authenticated copies of Transcript of Records, CSC/PRC ratings, and certificates.

04

Submit to HRMO

File your documents at the Municipal HRMO office or apply online through this portal.
