Municipal Revenue Code Standards

Regulatory Framework & Tax Schedules.

Legal tax brackets, incentive schedules, and procedural compliance for Tumauini enterprises.

Graduated Business Tax

Governed by Section 143 of the Local Government Code. Local business tax rates apply progressively according to audited gross receipts.

Gross Receipts Bracket Statutory Rate
Below ₱400,000 (Retail) Graduated Scale (2.0%)
Exceeding ₱400,000 1.0% on excess
Wholesalers / Trading 0.50% of gross
Financial / Lending 0.50% of gross interest
Cross-checked with BIR Form 1701 / 1702 Q4 filings.

BOSS Calendar & Surcharges

The Business One-Stop Shop (BOSS) operates January 1 to 20 at the Municipal Cultural Center. Surcharges accrue thereafter:

Jan 1 – Jan 20: 10% Discount on LBT
Jan 21 Onwards: 25% Late Surcharge
Monthly Interest: 2% Monthly Penalty
Early renewal ensures instant release of Mayor's validation stickers.

BMBE Tax Exemption (RA 9178)

Enterprises with total assets not exceeding ₱3,000,000 (excluding land) are eligible for Barangay Micro Business Enterprise incentives.

✓ 100% Exemption from Local Business Tax (LBT)
✓ Exemption from statutory income tax (BIR)
✓ Priority access to municipal credit facilities
Certificate of Authority issued through DTI and validated by BPLO.
