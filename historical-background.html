National Cultural Treasure

Historical background of Tumauini.

From its 1707 founding to the architectural marvel of San Matias Church, explore over three centuries of cultural heritage along the Cagayan River.

National Cultural Treasure · UNESCO Tentative List

Parish Church of San Matias

Constructed between 1783 and 1805 under Dominican friar Domingo Forto, San Matias Church represents the zenith of Philippine Spanish-era red clay brick masonry. Renowned for its distinctive cylindrical bell tower, the complex blends baroque ornamentation with intricate mudéjar influences.

  • Cylindrical Bell Tower: The only tiered circular brick campanile built in the Philippine archipelago.
  • Carved Brick Motifs: Over 1,000 uniquely numbered terra cotta tiles forming rosettes, cherubs, and garlands.
  • National Recognition: Declared a National Cultural Treasure on February 24, 1989.

Civic Chronology

Historical epochs and milestone events.

1707

Founding as Dominican Mission Settlement

Fray Francisco Nuñez established Tumauini as a Dominican mission outpost among the Gaddang and Ibanag inhabitants along the Pinacanauan and Cagayan river convergence.

1751

Township Declaration & Political Independence

Tumauini was officially separated from the administrative jurisdictions of Cabagan and Ilagan, establishing its own municipal cabecera and pueblo charter.

1783

Commencement of San Matias Brick Church

Fr. Domingo Forto initiated the construction of the monumental brick church, engaging master artisan masons from Lal-lo and local pottery kilns.

1805

Completion of the Cylindrical Campanile

The iconic four-tiered cylindrical bell tower was completed by Fr. Forto's brother, standing as an enduring symbol of Northern Luzon sacred architecture.

1882

Agrarian & Tobacco Monopoly Expansion

The town emerged as a cornerstone of the Tabacalera agro-industrial estate, developing fertile river plains for premier cigar-leaf and staple corn cultivation.

1945

Post-War Civic Reconstruction & Liberation

Allied forces and Cagayan Valley guerilla units liberated the municipality, followed by the systematic restoration of municipal civil records and public infrastructure.

1989 – 2026

National Treasure Designation & Smart City Era

Declared a National Cultural Treasure, Tumauini entered the 21st century modernizing its agrarian economy while preserving its historic soul and environmental watersheds.

Living Heritage

Preserving culture, riverways, and ancestral traditions.

Brick-Making Tradition

Ancestral artisanal kiln technology and clay molding techniques passed down through generations of Tumauineño potters.

Pinacanauan River Life

Vital river artery sustaining municipal agriculture, indigenous fishing practices, and natural biodiversity sanctuaries.

Patronal Feast of San Matias

Celebrated every February 24th, uniting 46 barangays in thanksgiving, cultural pageantry, and communal solidarity.
