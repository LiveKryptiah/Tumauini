Citizen Redress & Transparency

Right to Efficient Public Service.

Under Republic Act 11032, you have the right to transparent fees, zero-hidden steps, and timely service completion. Any officer who fails to deliver within the prescribed Citizen’s Charter time may be reported immediately to our Public Assistance and Complaints Desk (PACD) or national hotlines.
