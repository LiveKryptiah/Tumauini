Municipal Ordinance No. 2024-03 Protocols

Early Warning Threshold Tiers & Civic Actions.

Standardized four-stage disaster response protocols enforced across all 46 barangays.

Stage 01 • Green Tier

Code White (Normal)

River levels stable; routine OpCen standby and continuous telemetry logging.

Action: Unrestricted normal operations across residential and agricultural zones.
Stage 02 • Yellow Tier

Advisory Alert

Rivers reach 75% capacity; barangay tanod patrols monitor vulnerable riverbanks.

Action: Fisherfolk advisories issued; floodgates and drainage canals inspected.
Stage 03 • Orange Tier

Preemptive Warning

Overtopping threat detected; voluntary evacuation initiated for low-lying families.

Action: Evacuation shelters open; relief packs pre-positioned; sirens sound alert.
Stage 04 • Red Tier

Critical Evacuation

Forced evacuation enforced by PNP and Rescue 3325; river flood sirens active.

Action: Mandatory evacuation; watercraft deployed; curfew enforced in hazard zones.
