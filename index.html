Discover Tumauini.

Explore the places, stories, culture, and communities that make Tumauini unique.

San Matias Apostol Parish Church

A National Cultural Treasure renowned for the nation's only cylindrical brick belfry, ornamented with hand-carved terracotta baroque motifs and UNESCO Tentative List recognition.

Agricultural Heartland & Agrarian Abundance

Fertile river floodplains sustaining progressive farming barangays, premier yellow corn harvests, organic palay, and high-value agro-industrial enterprise.

Camp Samal & Scenic Valley Landscapes

Historic military encampment and ecotourism reserve boasting commanding ridge views of the Sierra Madre foothills, trekking routes, and serene civic picnic grounds.

What do you need?

Direct municipal assistance & civic processing.

Find the municipal service or information you need. Access verified frontline clearances, vital civil registrations, property assessment, business permits, and civic welfare support.

Business Permit

Registrations, renewals, and BPLO clearances.

BPLO Frontline Processing: 1–2 Days BOSS Window 1

Frontline registration and statutory clearances for commercial and industrial operations in Tumauini.

Key Requirements:

  • DTI Registration Certificate (Sole Prop) or SEC Articles of Incorporation
  • Barangay Business Clearance from the host barangay
  • Occupancy Permit, Valid Lease Contract, or Tax Declaration
  • Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (FSIC) from BFP Tumauini

Civil Registry

Birth, marriage, and death records.

Local Civil Registrar Same-Day Release LCR Window 3

Official registration, certified true copy issuance, and PSA endorsements for vital life records.

Key Requirements:

  • Valid Government-issued ID of registrant or authorized relative
  • Certificate of Live Birth, Marriage, or Death application slip
  • Barangay Certification of Residency (for delayed registrations)
  • Notarized Affidavit of Delayed Registration (if applicable)

Real Property

Tax declarations, assessments, and payments.

Municipal Assessor & Treasury 15–30 Mins Treasury Windows 1-2

Real property tax declarations, appraisal index checks, property transfer endorsements, and RPT payments.

Key Requirements:

  • Previous Official Receipt (O.R.) or Tax Declaration copy
  • Transfer Certificate of Title (TCT / OCT) for land transfers
  • Deed of Absolute Sale, Extrajudicial Settlement, or Donation
  • BIR Certificate Authorizing Registration (CAR)

Local Clearance

Mayor's clearances and certifications.

Mayor's Office Same-Day Release Clearance Counter

Frontline verification and official clearances for employment, local scholarship, and civic certifications.

Key Requirements:

  • Barangay Clearance for Employment or General Purpose
  • PNP Police Clearance from Tumauini Station
  • Valid Government Photo Identification
  • Community Tax Certificate (Cedula) for current fiscal year

Building Permit

Building, zoning, and occupancy approvals.

Municipal Engineering Office 3–5 Working Days 2nd Floor

Comprehensive engineering reviews, zoning clearance, structural evaluations, and occupancy certification.

Key Requirements:

  • 5 Sets of Architectural & Structural Plans (Signed & Sealed)
  • Land Title (TCT / OCT) or Proof of Ownership / Notarized Lease
  • Locational & Zoning Clearance from MPDO
  • Fire Safety Evaluation Clearance (FSEC) from BFP Tumauini

Community Services

Senior Citizen, PWD, and MSWDO aid.

MSWDO Frontline Same-Day Intake Welfare Wing

Frontline social welfare assistance, crisis financial support, PWD / Senior IDs, and family development aid.

Key Requirements:

  • Barangay Certificate of Indigency from host barangay
  • Medical Abstract / Doctor's Prescription (for medical aid)
  • Death Certificate and Funeral Contract (for burial assistance)
  • Valid Government IDs of beneficiary and authorized claimant

Request a Document

Official certifications and true copies.

Municipal Records 1 Working Day Secretariat Desk

Retrieval of certified true copies of municipal resolutions, ordinances, and official executive documents.

Key Requirements:

  • Formal Document Request Slip stating specific purpose
  • Valid Government Photo Identification
  • Proof of Legal Interest (for sensitive municipal records)
  • Official Receipt for statutory archival certification fee
